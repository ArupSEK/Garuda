Key recommendations

  1. Prioritize confirmed P1/P2 and CISA KEV findings.
  2. Restrict externally exposed administrative services to approved management paths.
  3. Remediate weak TLS/SSH configurations and remove unsupported services.
  4. Retest after remediation and compare against this baseline.

Limitations

This point-in-time assessment used safe external observations and excluded exploitation, brute force, denial-of-service, credential attacks, and intrusive fuzzing. Firewalls, CDNs, rate limits, and unavailable checks can affect visibility. Findings may require manual validation.
