# Authorized Use Policy

Use this software only against systems you own or have explicit written authorization to assess. The written authorization must identify the asset owner, approved IP/CIDR scope, exclusions, dates, scan profile, permitted window, rate expectations, and emergency contact.

Operators are responsible for applicable law, customer rules, cloud-provider policies, third-party hosting restrictions, monitoring notifications, and coordinated stop procedures. Begin with Quick Discovery, honor conservative rate limits, and monitor availability.

Prohibited uses include exploitation, brute force, password spraying, credential stuffing, denial-of-service, persistence, payload deployment, destructive modification, unrestricted fuzzing, evasion, IP rotation to bypass controls, and scanning outside approved scope.

Findings do not authorize access. Handle evidence as confidential customer information and follow responsible-disclosure requirements for unexpected third-party exposure.
